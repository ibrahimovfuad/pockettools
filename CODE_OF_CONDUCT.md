# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to use, discuss and contribute to this project, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We want this to be a friendly, respectful and harassment-free place for everybody.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, open an issue in this repository, or — if the matter is private — contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
