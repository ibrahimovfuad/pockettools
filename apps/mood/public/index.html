Rate your day in one tap

Move the slider from 0 to 10 and save. Add a short note if you like — that’s it.

See how your mood changes

Weekly, monthly and yearly charts show your average, your best days and how you compare to the last period.

A gentle daily reminder

Pick an hour and get one notification — only if you haven’t rated the day yet.

Private, free, no ads

Your ratings sync to your Google account and are visible only to you. No ads, no tracking — and it’s open source.

Before you start

Your mood entries and notes are stored in the cloud (Google Firebase) so they sync between your devices. You can export or delete them at any time in Account & data.

Mood entries can reveal how you feel, which the law treats as sensitive. Only you can see them. No ads, no tracking.
