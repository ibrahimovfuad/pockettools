Build good habits

Tick off habits every day or a few times a week. Set goals like “8 glasses of water”.

Keep your streak going

Streaks count your days in a row. Scheduled days off don’t break them.

Break bad habits too

Every day counts as clean by default. Only mark a slip — and watch the clean days add up.

History, completion rates and perfect days. Private, ad-free and open source.
